🌿 Kanyakumari Quality Coir

Direct Factory Processing in South India

Chinnathambi Coir Fibre (CCF) is located in the coastal coconut belt of Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu. Our facility is equipped with modern dye boilers, steel-pin hackling floors, and large open-air sun drying yards.

Our team brings over 35 years of hands-on experience in selecting raw coconut husks, hot black dyeing, and careful manual hand-packing of 52kg - 56kg gunny bales for brush and broom manufacturers in Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Delhi, Kanpur, Kolkata, Bangalore, and all across India.
